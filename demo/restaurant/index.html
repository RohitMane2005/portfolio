Our Story

A Labour of Love Since 2018

Ember & Oak was born from a simple belief: the best food comes from the best ingredients, handled with care and cooked with fire. Chef Marco Rossi trained across Europe before returning home to build something lasting.

Our dining room seats 60 guests in an atmosphere that's warm without being stuffy — exposed oak beams, candlelight, and the quiet crackle of the hearth.
